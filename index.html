How it's made

From the field to your kitchen, in three honest steps.

We film the entire process and post it — so you never have to take our word for it.

STEP 01

Sun-dried naturally

Fresh red chillies from local Assam farms are laid out and dried under open sun — the traditional way, with no forced dehydration or chemical drying agents.

STEP 02

Stone-ground in small batches

We grind in small batches so nothing sits around losing flavour, and so every packet is genuinely fresh, not stockpiled months in advance.

STEP 03

Sealed & sent to you

Packed straight into 250gm, 300gm, 500gm and 1kg packets, no fillers or anti-caking agents added, and shipped or hand-delivered near you.
